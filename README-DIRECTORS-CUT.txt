WHAT CHANGED
============
1. Unified motion grammar
   Every post-prologue scene now follows the same sequence:
   arrive -> settle -> hold -> release.

2. Smoother scroll-linked animation
   The motion engine now interpolates toward the real scroll position rather than mapping
   every wheel/trackpad movement directly to transforms.

3. Removed competing animation directions
   No alternating left/right card fly-ins, diagonal title moves or separate scene gimmicks.
   Titles, copy, media and CTAs all use restrained vertical movement plus a small camera push.

4. Calmer colour choreography
   Ivory and ink are the two main film stocks. Blue is the connective accent.
   Coral and lime remain as small punctuation rather than giving every scene a different identity.

5. Shared layout rail
   Major content aligns to the same maximum width and safe margins from scene to scene.

6. Work montage simplified
   Project cards stagger gently upward and images use a small consistent zoom instead of panning
   in different directions.

7. Photography simplified
   The two images retain depth but use fixed subtle rotations and small opposing vertical drift.

8. No blur
   Scroll/reveal animation does not use blur.
