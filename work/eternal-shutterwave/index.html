BRAND IDENTITY · WEB DESIGN · VISUAL DIRECTION

Eternal Shutterwave.

A photography brand created around emotion, memory and visual storytelling — extending from the digital experience into print and social media communication.

ROLE Brand & UI/UX Designer
PROJECT Photography Brand
DELIVERABLES Website · Print · Social
FOCUS Identity · Storytelling

01 / CHALLENGE

Photography captures a moment. The brand should capture a feeling.

Eternal Shutterwave needed more than a gallery. The identity had to feel recognisable across the website, printed collateral and social communication.

01Build recognition

Create one visual language across every touchpoint.

02Let imagery lead

Keep photography at the emotional centre of the brand.

03Create a mood

Balance cinematic, premium and approachable visual direction.

02 / BRAND DIRECTION

One identity. Multiple expressions.

MY ROLE Brand Identity UI/UX & Web Design Brand Collateral Social Media Design

MOTIF

05 / KEY DECISIONS

Consistency creates recognition.

01

Keep photography dominant.

The interface supports the work rather than competing with it.

02

Repeat the wave motif.

The red wave creates a recognisable visual thread across every format.

03

Design a system, not a logo.

Typography, colour, imagery and graphic elements behave consistently together.

06

OUTCOME & REFLECTION

A brand becomes real when it travels.

Eternal Shutterwave helped me think beyond a single interface and consider how one visual idea can survive across digital, physical and promotional formats.

I would continue expanding the identity into additional applications and explore a dedicated responsive mobile website experience.
