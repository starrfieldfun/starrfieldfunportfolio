SERVICE 01 ↗

UI/UX
Consultancy.

For an existing website or application that needs a fresh, constructive perspective. I review the experience, identify what works and what creates friction, then explain practical next steps.

WHAT WE'LL WORK ON

  • Review of the existing pages or user flows agreed in the brief
  • Feedback on usability, navigation, content hierarchy and visual clarity

WHAT YOU'LL RECEIVE

  • A structured review report or walkthrough
  • Clear, actionable suggestions for your team

DELIVERABLE / A UX review and improvement recommendations. A redesign or development is not included.

SERVICE 02 ↗

UI/UX Design
in Figma.

For a new digital experience or an existing interface that needs redesigning. I turn the agreed requirements into thoughtful, responsive designs your team can review and build from.

WHAT WE'LL WORK ON

  • Discovery and clarification of goals, users and scope
  • Information architecture, flows and wireframes where needed
  • High-fidelity desktop and mobile interface designs
  • Interactive prototype and reusable components where appropriate

WHAT YOU'LL RECEIVE

  • Organised Figma files and agreed screen designs
  • Prototype and component specifications as scoped
  • Developer handoff notes for implementation

DELIVERABLE / An agreed Figma design package. Website development and deployment are not included.

SERVICE 03 ↗

Website Design
& Development.

For a website you want designed and built, not just handed off as mockups. I work from requirements and design through to implementation, testing and launch assistance.

WHAT WE'LL WORK ON

  • Website structure, UI/UX and responsive visual design
  • Front-end development of the agreed pages and interactions
  • Desktop and mobile testing, accessibility and SEO basics
  • Deployment support and a practical handover

WHAT YOU'LL RECEIVE

  • Responsive, working website within the agreed scope
  • Source files and agreed handover documentation
  • Deployment assistance on the agreed hosting platform
